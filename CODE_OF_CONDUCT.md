# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Eternaldad is a community built by single fathers, for single fathers. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and supportive environment for everyone, regardless of:

- Age or experience as a single parent
- Geographic location
- Income level
- Family size or structure
- Race, ethnicity, or nationality
- Gender identity
- Sexual orientation
- Religion or belief
- Ability or disability
- Professional background

**We are not here to judge. We are here to share systems that work.**

---

## Expected Behavior

### Be Specific

Vague criticism helps no one. If you see something that needs improvement:
- State the specific problem
- Explain why it's a problem
- Suggest a better approach if you have one

**Instead of:** "This is not good."  
**Say:** "This step assumes the child is verbal, but what about a two-year-old who is nonverbal? Can you add an alternative approach for that situation?"

### Be Respectful

Single fathers are doing hard things under real pressure. Even when you disagree:
- Assume good intent
- Address the idea, not the person
- Acknowledge the effort, even if you see gaps

**Instead of:** "This is a stupid system that won't work."  
**Say:** "I tried this approach in my household and it broke down when Kennedy had a bad day at school. Here's what worked better for us in that situation."

### Be Honest

Tell the truth about what works and what doesn't:
- Share what succeeded
- Share what failed
- Share the conditions where your system holds and where it breaks

**Not this:** "Everything is amazing and my household runs perfectly."  
**This:** "This system works when the children are well-rested and fed. It breaks down when someone is sick or we've had a disrupted schedule. Here's what I do in that situation."

### Be Humble

You do not know someone else's household pressure.
- What works for you might not work for him
- What failed for you might be exactly what he needs
- Different situations require different systems

**Not this:** "Any father who doesn't use my system is doing it wrong."  
**This:** "This system works for us because we have a predictable schedule and my children respond well to structure. It might not work if your situation is different."

### Be Kind

We are all exhausted. We are all trying. We are all scared sometimes.

When someone shares a struggle:
- Acknowledge how hard it is
- Offer a system or suggestion if you have one
- Say "I've been there" if you have

**Not this:** Silence or dismissal or "just try harder."  
**This:** "That is really hard. Here's what helped me with a similar situation. It might help you too."

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do **not** tolerate:

### Harassment
- Personal attacks or insults
- Threats or intimidation
- Unwanted sexual advances or comments
- Discrimination based on identity
- Repeated, unwanted contact after being asked to stop

### Disrespect
- Dismissing someone's household or family structure
- Shaming a single father for his situation or his choices
- Making assumptions about someone's parenting based on their income, location, or background
- Gatekeeping or gatekeeping language ("real fathers do this")

### Bad Faith

- Deliberately misrepresenting what someone said
- Trolling or baiting for conflict
- Posting solely to criticize without offering alternatives
- Using the platform to promote unrelated products or services

### Abuse of Process

- Spamming Issues or Discussions
- Creating duplicate or frivolous Issues/PRs
- Using the repository for purposes other than building systems for single fathers

## What To Do If Something Isn't Right

### If You See a Violation

1. **Do not engage in public escalation.** Do not respond to harassment or bad behavior in the Discussion or Issue. This often makes it worse.

2. **Report it directly.** Email the maintainer:
   - **What happened** — Be specific. Quote if relevant.
   - **Who it involved** — Usernames or names
   - **When it happened** — Date and time if you have it
   - **Links** — To the Issue, Discussion, or Comment where it happened
   - **Why it concerns you** — How does this violate the Code of Conduct?

3. **We will investigate and respond.**

### If You're in Conflict with Someone

1. **Assume good intent** — They probably meant well and missed something
2. **Clarify privately** — If you have a question, ask directly
3. **If that doesn't work** — Report it to the maintainer

---

## Enforcement

### For Minor Violations

The maintainer will:
- Have a private conversation with the person
- Explain what the specific issue is
- Ask them to adjust their behavior
- Move forward

Most people will correct course here.

### For Repeated or Serious Violations

The maintainer will:
- Warn the person privately
- Explain the specific violation and the impact
- Set a clear expectation for change
- Monitor for improvement

### For Severe Violations

- Removal from the repository
- Blocking from future participation
- No refund if they had purchased products (though this is rare)

**Severe violations include:**
- Harassment or threats
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexual advances
- Deliberate, sustained abuse

---

## Special Cases

### Disagreement Is Not Violation

It is **okay** to:
- Disagree with a system
- Say "this didn't work for me"
- Suggest an alternative approach
- Question the research behind a claim
- Ask for clarification

Healthy disagreement strengthens the community. It is the **manner** that matters.

### Criticism of Ideas, Not People

It is **okay** to:
- "This system assumes the child can read, but what about a five-year-old?"
- "I tried this and it broke down because..."
- "The research says X, but this system assumes Y. Can you clarify?"

It is **not okay** to:
- "You're stupid if you think this works"
- "Anyone who uses this is a bad father"
- "This is the dumbest idea I've ever heard"

### Scope Boundaries

This repository is for **systems single fathers build and test in their households.**

It is **not** for:
- General debates about parenting philosophy
- Political or religious arguments
- Unrelated life advice
- Spam or self-promotion

If a Discussion goes outside scope, the maintainer will redirect it or close it.

---

## Our Responsibility

The maintainers and leaders of Eternaldad commit to:

- **Modeling the behavior we expect** — Specific, honest, respectful, humble
- **Enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly** — Consistently and without favoritism
- **Being responsive** — If someone reports a violation, we take it seriously
- **Being transparent** — We will explain our decisions when asked
- **Protecting privacy** — We will not publicly shame or call out individuals

---

## The Why

Single fathers already face real pressure from the world. **We will not add pressure here.**

This repository is a safe space to share what works, what doesn't, and to help each other build better systems.

That only happens if we are specific, honest, respectful, and kind to each other.
